# ORG-MARKET-1 — CPU Free Agency and Payroll Identity

## Objective

Make CPU clubs pursue free agents and allocate payroll in line with organization identity while obeying real budgets, roster needs, contracts, and market rules.

## Source-first checkpoint

Map free-agent valuation, offer construction, budget/payroll ownership, team timeline, roster needs, difficulty modifiers, and tests. Identify any hidden budget or information advantages before adding identity.

## Design

- Identity affects spending posture, age/term/risk preference, positional priority, and patience.
- No free money, hidden ratings, forced acceptance, discounted contracts, or outcome bonuses.
- All choices remain within existing market and roster legality.
- Explanations/news may describe factual choices, not claim motives unsupported by actual inputs.

## Proof

- budget conservation and roster legality;
- no hidden information/discount invariant;
- constructed profiles produce different valid offer strategies;
- same-seed reproducibility;
- market inflation and payroll-distribution soak;
- full gates.

## Scope cut line

Free agency and payroll posture only. No trade/development work, market rewrite, new economic model, or new route.

## Done

CPU clubs spend differently for explainable, fair reasons while budgets, contracts, and deterministic market behavior remain sound.
