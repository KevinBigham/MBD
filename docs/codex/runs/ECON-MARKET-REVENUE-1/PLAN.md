`market + completed record + playoff berth -> modeled gross revenue -> owner allocation -> exact durable next-season budget -> visible consequence`

## Decision log

1. Exact uninitialized `season_review` Advance/Skip is the sole annual owner.
2. Formula is explicit market baseline plus bounded final-record effect plus
   fixed berth bump, then canonical owner-archetype allocation.
3. Satisfaction, fan sentiment, GM philosophy/spending override, difficulty,
   prior budget, payroll, and tax are excluded.
4. Existing owner financial fields store the durable allocation; no revenue,
   cash, or attendance ledger and no schema bump are added.
5. Existing archives describe the prior season's raw budget, not revenue or a
   user-only difficulty overlay.
6. Old saves already past season review, and compact saves with no league-wide
   postseason artifact, wait until the next factual completed season; no
   backfill or invented statement.
7. The 4x4 study is item-15 evidence; item 18 retains the 30-season soak.

## Completion conditions and rollback

All MRB acceptance rows require observed focused, root, browser, and review
evidence. Completion also requires a scoped commit on local `main`, unchanged
protected user files, and no push/deploy/tag. Before commit, roll back only this
slice's owned paths; after commit, revert the single item-15 commit. With no
schema change, rollback requires no migration or historical repair.
